# JobBridge Backlink Strategy

## Goals

- Earn relevant, trustworthy links from Nigerian career, hiring, education, startup, and business ecosystems.
- Build authority for priority pages: `/jobs`, `/recruiter`, `/providers`, `/ai-resume`, `/pricing`, and `/blog`.
- Avoid paid link farms, private blog networks, comment spam, or irrelevant directories.

## Target Sources

- University career centers and alumni associations: publish JobBridge as a job search and career tool for students and graduates.
- Professional communities: HR, product, engineering, design, marketing, and business groups with resource pages.
- Startup and SME directories: list JobBridge as a hiring, advertising, and talent marketplace.
- Training providers: co-publish career guides and link to AI resume and career pages.
- Local business publications: pitch stories about Nigerian hiring trends and JobBridge marketplace data.
- Podcast/newsletter partnerships: provide guest insights on hiring, remote work, and employability.

## Linkable Assets

- Career guides on the blog: resume optimization, salary negotiation, interview prep, and AI hiring explainers.
- Data reports: monthly Nigerian job market snapshots using anonymized platform trends.
- Employer resources: hiring checklist, job description templates, and recruitment pricing explainer.
- Service provider directory pages: category-focused resources for finding verified professionals.

## Outreach Cadence

- Week 1: Build a list of 50 relevant websites and contacts.
- Week 2: Publish or refresh 3 linkable blog resources.
- Week 3: Send 25 personalized outreach emails and 10 partnership messages.
- Week 4: Follow up once, track responses, and add successful links to a backlink log.
- Monthly: Repeat with new content and review link quality in Search Console.

## Quality Rules

- Prioritize relevance, editorial placement, and real human audiences.
- Use varied anchor text such as "JobBridge", "AI resume builder", "job marketplace in Nigeria", and natural article titles.
- Reject links from spammy sites, hacked pages, unrelated gambling/adult/pharma content, or domains with thin copied content.
- Track source URL, target page, anchor text, contact, date, and status.

## Success Metrics

- 10 to 20 high-quality referring domains in the first 90 days.
- Growth in branded search impressions and non-branded clicks to priority pages.
- Search Console index coverage remains clean for sitemap pages.
- Referral traffic converts into signups, job views, applications, or recruiter leads.
